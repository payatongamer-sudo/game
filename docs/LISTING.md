# Melty listing (draft)

**Title:** Brewing Point

**Tagline:** Minecraft's Brewing Stand comes to Hyland Point. Nether Wart first, then brew.

**Description:**

Brew your product the Minecraft way. Buy a Minecraft Brewing Stand at Dan's Hardware and Minecraft's brewing
ingredients at the Gas-Mart. Brew Nether Wart in first to make an Awkward batch. Then Sugar, Redstone Dust,
Glowstone Dust, Rabbit's Foot and the rest add Energizing, Electrifying, Glowing, Anti-Gravity and more. Fermented
Spider Eye corrupts what's already there: Energizing turns Sedating, Bright-Eyed turns Sneaky. The stand brews three
bottles at a time and plays Minecraft's own brewing sound when it's done.

The stand's model, the icons, the names and the sounds all come from your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition
(1.14+, any launcher). Works in Schedule I co-op for up to 4 players; everyone needs the mashup and their own Minecraft.

**Games:** Schedule I (`pc-schedule-i`, primary) · Minecraft: Java Edition (`minecraft-java`, companion)

**Players:** co-op, up to 4 (Schedule I's Steam co-op: the host invites, friends accept)
